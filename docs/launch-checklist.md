## After launch

1. Set final canonical origin and `NEXT_PUBLIC_INDEXABLE=true`, rebuild and inspect HTML/robots/sitemap on the live domain.
2. Verify domain ownership in Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools; submit `/sitemap.xml`.
3. Align verified name/address/phone with the business's Google Business Profile and Facebook. Do not create duplicate profiles.
4. Validate structured data against visible facts; FAQ markup does not guarantee rich results.
5. Check mobile field performance after traffic arrives. p75 targets: LCP ≤2.5 s, INP ≤200 ms and CLS ≤0.1. Record actual results, never substitute a lab estimate for field percentiles.
6. Add analytics only with an agreed privacy/consent policy. Never log enquiry details in analytics.
7. Replace illustrative assets with approved photography and meaningful image descriptions. Review page titles/FAQs as the verified range develops.
