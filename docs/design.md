# Art direction and content map

The final direction follows the user's correction: **black, charcoal and muted gold**, consistent with NICE's supplied metallic logo. Warm clay remains in the imagery, not a white page theme. Backgrounds are quiet, dark and predominantly neutral; borders and typography use controlled brass/gold. The raster logo is cropped from the original source screenshot, not an AI-generated vector master.

Cormorant Garamond provides architectural editorial headings; Manrope supplies legible navigation, body copy and controls. Both Fontsource packages are self-hosted and distributed under SIL Open Font License 1.1; see the included font licences. The decorative N favicon is a text fallback, not an official trademark master.

Home moves from material identity to discovery, visual texture, inspiration, service enquiries and contact. A staggered two-column collection rhythm replaces repeated feature cards. Individual products and service questions have real routes. There are no reviews, counters, awards, fabricated stock or checkout.

The logo's black/gold palette is the visual authority. Source marketing slogans are used sparingly; original website headings are copy, not claims of a new official tagline. The footer does not use a registered-trademark symbol.

A user-selectable warm limestone light theme complements the default black and gold. The masthead and footer retain the dark brand identity in both modes. The preference persists locally, with a nonce-protected pre-paint script to prevent a theme flash.

The hero image responds to scrolling with a restrained vertical shift and scale. Gallery images reveal from the centre using Motion useScroll/useTransform and the requested inset clip path. Reduced-motion preferences disable scroll effects and transitions. Server-rendered images remain visible without JavaScript, and keyboard focus reveals the whole image. There is no autoplay video cost, background tracking, scroll hijacking or hidden content requiring animation.

The Lens adaptation preserves the supplied interaction idea while avoiding duplicate children, pointer-triggered React rerenders and interfering layers. Pointer movement updates CSS coordinates once per animation frame. Image magnification cannot create detail absent from a low-resolution master; the full-screen view and labels communicate that limitation.

The only project-like imagery currently on the public-facing pages is labelled illustrative. The original charcoal-roof source is retained as evidence and has not silently become a claimed orange-tile NICE project. Once approved real job photography arrives, add a separate verified project content type with location/description/permission fields instead of relabelling illustrations.

Roofmart (https://www.roofmartlk.com/, reviewed 25 October 2026) was used as a reference for navigable material categories, separate detail pages and direct enquiry paths. Its content, photography, claims and visual identity were not copied.
